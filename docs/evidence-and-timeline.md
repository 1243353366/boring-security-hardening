# Evidence and timeline worksheet

Use this worksheet to organize a defensive investigation. Follow your organization’s evidence handling and privacy procedures. Do not access systems or accounts unless you are authorized. For a serious incident, ask qualified responders how evidence should be preserved.

## Working rules

- Preserve original artifacts where practical; work from copies when possible.
- Record where each item came from, who collected it, and when (including time zone).
- Do not edit, rename, or redistribute originals unless your process requires it.
- Keep sensitive evidence access-limited; do not put credentials, recovery codes, or unnecessary personal data in notes.
- Label each statement **confirmed**, **inferred**, or **unknown**. Record contradictory evidence too.
